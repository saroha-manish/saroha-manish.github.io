FWA RISK-RANKING MODEL

Project Overview

Developed an explainable machine learning model to rank medical billing providers by fraud, waste, and abuse (FWA) risk in auto casualty claims. Using approximately 806 million bill lines from multiple insurance carriers, the model scored 298,000 providers and gave investigators a prioritized worklist with risk tiers and reasons behind each score. It achieved a 0.758 PR-AUC and a 68.8% hit rate among the top 10% of ranked providers.

Key Features

  • Cross-Carrier Risk Detection: Identified billing patterns across multiple insurers that may be difficult to detect from a single carrier’s data.
  • Provider Risk Ranking: Assigned providers a risk score and tier to help investigators prioritize reviews.
  • Explainable Predictions: Used SHAP to show the strongest factors contributing to each provider’s score.
  • Operational Integration: Produced a regularly updated worklist for Special Investigations Unit (SIU) teams.

Technologies and Tools

  • Programming Language: Python
  • Data Platform: Snowflake and SQL
  • Machine Learning: XGBoost, LightGBM, Random Forest, Logistic Regression
  • Explainability: SHAP
  • Model Development: Feature engineering, cross-validation, and hyperparameter tuning

Problem Statement

Investigators had limited capacity to review large volumes of medical billing activity. Fraud reviews were often reactive, and billing across different carriers made suspicious provider behavior harder to identify. The business needed a consistent way to prioritize providers for investigation before further payments were made.

Solution Approach

Combined five years of billing data at the provider Tax ID level and developed behavioral features covering billing volume, pricing, procedures, geography, and timing. Compared several machine learning algorithms and selected XGBoost for the final model. The model generates provider risk scores, review tiers, and SHAP-based reasons that investigators can examine alongside the underlying bill history.

Results

  • Strong Risk Ranking: Achieved a PR-AUC of 0.758 and ROC-AUC of 0.951 on held-out data.
  • Focused Investigations: The top 10% of ranked providers contained 72.8% of SIU-flagged providers.
  • Higher-Yield Worklist: The top decile had a 68.8% hit rate, representing 7.3× lift over the sample baseline.
  • Explainable Review: Each score included reasons to help investigators assess potential billing concerns.

Challenges

Key challenges included inconsistent provider identifiers, missing data, class imbalance, and the possibility that some unflagged providers had not yet been investigated. The project also required controls to reduce overfitting and ensure the model’s scores reflected provider behavior rather than differences in carrier workflows.

Future Work

Future improvements include using completed investigation outcomes as new training labels, expanding carrier-level data quality checks, and monitoring model performance as billing patterns change. Investigator feedback can also help refine risk tiers and make the worklist more useful over time.
