AdvisorFit — AI-Powered Recruitment Intelligence

A top performer propensity model and candidate reporting platform that turns recruitment-stage information into practical guidance for financial advisor recruiters.

Project Overview

Developed AdvisorFit for Sun Life Philippines to identify financial advisor candidates with a higher likelihood of becoming top performers. The project combined historical recruitment and performance data, supervised machine learning, business outcome validation, and AI-generated candidate reports.

A curated dataset of approximately 15,150 historical applications supported model development. The solution scores candidates using information available during recruitment, then presents the results as propensity scores, decile rankings, and recruiter-friendly guidance.

AdvisorFit was designed as a decision-support platform: recruiters use its signals to prioritize conversations, investigate candidate strengths and concerns, and plan early coaching. Final hiring decisions remain with Recruit Managers and business leaders.

Key Features

Top Performer Propensity

Estimates a candidate's likelihood of becoming a top-performing advisor from recruitment-stage profile and background data.

Business Decile Ranking

Groups candidates by model score so recruiters can identify higher-potential candidates without interpreting raw model outputs.

Recruitment Text Signals

Structures open-ended responses into features reflecting ambition, achievements, entrepreneurship, leadership, and career goals.

Outcome Validation

Compares score groups with early sales, first-year production, and 13-month retention to test whether rankings align with business results.

AI Candidate Reports

Uses structured candidate context and Claude Sonnet to draft summaries, positive signals, validation areas, interview questions, and guidance.

Human Review

Keeps recruiter judgment central and checks generated observations against the candidate's actual information.

Pilot Batch Workflow

Designed for weekly batch scoring and report generation for active candidates in the Lead-Based Platform (LBP).

Problem Statement

Recruitment teams had limited structured evidence at the application stage to distinguish candidates who might become successful advisors. Decisions depended heavily on individual recruiter experience, making large candidate pipelines difficult to prioritize consistently.

The business needed a way to combine available candidate information into an interpretable signal, test that signal against actual advisor outcomes, and give recruiters concrete questions to explore during interviews.

Solution Approach

Built SQL-based pipelines to combine candidate profiles, employment and education histories, questionnaire responses, sales production, performance categories, and advisor status. The model used only information available at recruitment time; future performance, post-hire attributes, direct identifiers, and other leakage-prone fields were excluded from scoring inputs.

  • Model Development: Benchmarked CatBoost, XGBoost, LightGBM, and AutoML experiments, selecting the final model based on ranking performance, stability, and usability.
  • Business Interpretation: Converted model scores into candidate deciles and higher, medium, and lower potential groups for recruiter use.
  • Sales Validation: Examined first 90-day AFYP, policy count, and days to first sale, alongside first 12-month production.
  • Retention Validation: Analyzed 13-month retention and cancellation using agreed advisor status definitions, including Active, Cancelled, Deceased, and Vested.
  • Report Generation: Used AWS Bedrock and Claude Sonnet to turn structured scores and candidate facts into readable reports with interview focus areas and recommendations.
  • Pilot Design: Planned batch scoring of active LBP candidates and Recruit Manager-specific report delivery as an initial operating model.

Results & Business Impact

  • Top-Decile Lift: The highest-scoring decile achieved approximately 2.04× lift over the overall top performer rate.
  • Candidate Separation: Approximately 27.8% of candidates in the highest-scoring decile became top performers, compared with an overall rate of approximately 13.6%.
  • Early Sales Alignment: Higher model-score groups showed stronger first 90-day AFYP and policy production than lower-scoring groups.
  • Retention Insight: Stronger early and first-year sales performance was associated with better 13-month retention and lower cancellation.
  • AFYP Opportunity Scenario: A replacement analysis estimated approximately 37M in additional AFYP if candidates in deciles 5–10 were replaced by previously uncoded candidates in the top-performing deciles 1–5 who also met the good-retention criteria. This is a modeled opportunity, not realized revenue.
  • Recruiter Guidance: Candidate reports translated model signals into strengths, validation areas, and interview questions that managers could act on.
  • Pilot Direction: Defined a batch-service approach for active LBP candidates, with broader application integration considered after pilot validation.

Business Value

AdvisorFit helps recruiters focus attention on promising candidates and examine specific uncertainties during interviews. The replacement analysis identified a potential 37M AFYP uplift under its candidate-selection and retention assumptions; actual gains require pilot validation.

Challenges

  • Data Leakage: Excluded performance outcomes, target categories, post-recruitment attributes, and identifiers that could overstate predictive quality.
  • Feature Consistency: Managed missing values, unknown categories, high-cardinality fields, and text-derived signals across historical and current candidates.
  • Metric Alignment: Reconciled first 3-month and first 12-month AFYP calculations and confirmed the business definitions behind performance categories.
  • Retention Logic: Aligned advisor status definitions before interpreting 13-month retention and cancellation trends.
  • Generated Report Quality: Refined prompts and reviewed reports to reduce unsupported claims, contradictions, and generic recommendations.
  • Operational Fit: Assessed batch delivery, LBP integration, and enterprise application options against cost, dependencies, and recruiter workflows.
Positioning Note: AdvisorFit supports candidate prioritization and interview preparation. Its score is a predictive signal, not a guarantee of future performance or an automated hiring decision. Recruit Managers and business leaders retain final judgment.
