AdvisorFit — AI-Powered Recruitment Intelligence

Current status: Pilot. Production rollout: Planned for January 2027.

Project Overview

Independently built AdvisorFit using 15,150 historical applications, owning stakeholder requirements, engineering implementation and pilot delivery.

The platform helps Recruit Managers prioritise candidates using propensity scores and actionable reports. A recruiter-assistance agent combines candidate profiles and model scores to highlight strengths, concerns and suggested interview questions before the interview call.

Candidate-scoring, PDF generation and delivery are integrated into agent workflows. The final output is a candidate-specific PDF report delivered to the respective Recruit Manager for review and interview preparation.

Business Problem

Recruit Managers needed to review application information, speak with candidates and reach a hiring decision. They needed a consistent way to prioritise conversations and identify relevant questions before each interview.

AdvisorFit brings candidate information, predicted top-performer propensity and interview guidance into one report, helping managers focus their review and candidate conversations.

Key Capabilities

Candidate Propensity Scoring

Uses recruitment-stage information to estimate a candidate’s likelihood of becoming a top-performing advisor.

Decile Ranking

Groups candidates by model score to support prioritisation of recruitment conversations.

Recruiter-Assistance Agent

Combines candidate profiles and model scores to highlight strengths, concerns and interview questions.

Candidate PDF Reports

Presents candidate information, scores and interview guidance in a PDF delivered to the respective Recruit Manager.

Recruitment-Stage Inputs

Integrates profile, employment, education, relationship and free-text data while excluding post-hire outcomes from scoring inputs.

Business Outcome Analysis

Assesses rankings against 90-day sales, 12-month AFYP and 13-month retention.

Candidate-to-Report Workflow

  1. Prepare Candidate Data: Integrate recruitment-stage information through SQL pipelines. Insufficient candidate data can prevent report generation.
  2. Generate Candidate Scores: Use the propensity model to produce candidate scores and decile rankings.
  3. Generate Interview Guidance: Combine candidate profiles and scores through the recruiter-assistance agent to highlight strengths, concerns and suggested interview questions.
  4. Create the PDF: Assemble candidate information, model outputs and interview guidance into a candidate-specific PDF.
  5. Deliver for Manager Review: Deliver the generated report to the respective Recruit Manager before the interview.

Business Outcome Validation

Analysed score rankings against 90-day sales, 12-month AFYP and 13-month retention using hypothesis tests and confidence intervals.

Communicated findings, assumptions and limitations to help stakeholders assess whether the rankings were relevant to business outcomes.

Scenario-Based Business Case

Modelled a potential CAD 37M AFYP uplift through what-if analysis, providing stakeholders with a quantified business case and documented candidate-selection assumptions.

Potential Opportunity

The CAD 37M AFYP uplift is a projected scenario requiring pilot validation. It is not realised revenue or an established production result.
