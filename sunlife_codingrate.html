AdvisorFit — Agent Recruitment and Top Performer Propensity Model

A data-driven recruitment intelligence solution designed to identify high-potential advisor candidates early, support recruiter decision-making, and validate candidate scores against real business outcomes.

Project Overview

Designed and developed AdvisorFit, an agent recruitment and top performer propensity model that helps recruitment teams identify high-potential advisor candidates early in the hiring process.

The model uses candidate profile, background, work history, education, family, motivation, and selected text-derived features to predict the probability of a recruit becoming a future top performer.

The solution is built as a decision-support tool for recruiters and managers. It helps them prioritize candidates, ask better interview questions, and plan early coaching interventions without replacing business judgment.

Key Features

Top Performer Propensity Scoring

Predicts the probability of a new recruit becoming a future top performer using application-stage data.

Business Decile Ranking

Ranks candidates into model-score deciles to create easy-to-understand high, medium, and low potential groups.

Early Sales Validation

Validates model deciles against first 90-day sales outcomes including AFYP, policy count, and days to first sale.

Retention Validation

Compares model deciles and actual sales deciles against 13-month retention and cancellation behavior.

Text Feature Engineering

Converts open-ended recruitment responses into structured features such as motivation, achievements, entrepreneurship, and career vision.

Explainable Candidate Report

Generates recruiter-friendly reports with probability score, strengths, risk areas, interview focus points, and final recommendation.

Pilot-Ready Batch Scoring

Supports batch scoring and candidate report generation for advisor candidates available in LBP.

Problem Statement

Recruitment teams often do not have enough structured early signals to identify which advisor candidates are more likely to become strong performers. Traditional assessment depends heavily on manual judgment and business experience, making it difficult to prioritize candidates consistently.

The business needed a data-driven solution that could support recruitment decisions, highlight candidate potential, and provide clear interview guidance while keeping recruiter judgment at the center of the process.

Solution Approach

Built a supervised classification model to predict whether a recruit is likely to become a top performer. The target was created from historical advisor performance categories, while leakage-prone fields such as actual first-year performance, target category, identifiers, and post-outcome variables were excluded from model training.

  • Curated Feature Set: Used stable and interpretable features covering demographics, work background, education, Sun Life relationship indicators, family background, and structured text-derived signals.
  • Probability Scoring: Converted model outputs into business-friendly top performer probability scores.
  • Decile-Based Interpretation: Grouped candidates into score deciles for easier business adoption.
  • Business Validation: Validated model deciles against first 90-day AFYP, policy count, days to first sale, first 12-month AFYP, and 13-month retention.
  • Recruiter Report Prototype: Created a Streamlit-based interface explaining positive signals, validation areas, interview recommendations, and final hiring guidance.

Results & Business Impact

  • Model Ranking Validation: Higher model-score deciles showed stronger early sales and better retention outcomes.
  • Early Sales Alignment: High model-score deciles generated higher first 90-day AFYP and policy volume compared with low model-score deciles.
  • Actual Sales Validation: Advisors ranked in higher actual first 12-month AFYP deciles showed stronger 13-month retention and lower cancellation.
  • First 90-Day Insight: Advisors with stronger first 3-month AFYP showed materially better 13-month retention, confirming early sales behavior as a strong business signal.
  • Top Performer Behavior Pattern: Actual top performers were more likely to make their first sale early and build policy volume within the first 90 days.
  • Business Adoption Direction: The business preferred a batch-service pilot using current advisor candidates available in LBP, with possible movement to an enterprise application after pilot validation.

Business Value

AdvisorFit converts recruitment data into actionable candidate intelligence, helping managers prioritize high-potential candidates, identify validation areas early, and make more consistent recruitment decisions.

Challenges

  • Data Leakage Prevention: Removed outcome-related and post-performance variables such as actual first-year performance, category fields, and direct identifiers from model training.
  • Feature Consistency: Handled missing values, unknown categories, text-derived features, and high-cardinality fields carefully to maintain model stability.
  • Metric Definition Alignment: Validated that first 3-month and first 12-month AFYP were calculated from the same policy source to avoid incorrect business interpretation.
  • Retention Logic: Worked with business to confirm advisor status definitions such as Active, Cancelled, Deceased, and Vested before calculating 13-month retention and cancellation.
  • Business Explainability: Translated model outputs into simple recruiter-friendly explanations, avoiding black-box score interpretation.
  • Production Planning: Evaluated enterprise app, LBP integration, and batch-service pilot options considering cost, feasibility, user experience, and technical dependencies.
Positioning Note: This project should be presented as a recruitment decision-support system, not as an automated hiring decision engine. The model supports prioritization, interview preparation, and early coaching planning while keeping final judgment with recruiters and business leaders.
